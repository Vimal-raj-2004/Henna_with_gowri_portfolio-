The Art & Heritage of Henna

What Is Henna?

Henna, or "mehindi," is a natural plant-based dye used for thousands of years across South Asian, Middle Eastern, and North African cultures. The leaves are ground into a fine powder, mixed with oils and other natural ingredients, and applied to the skin in intricate patterns.

Cultural Significance: Henna holds deep cultural meaning, particularly in wedding celebrations, religious festivals, and life milestones. It symbolizes joy, beauty, prosperity, and the bonds of family and community.
